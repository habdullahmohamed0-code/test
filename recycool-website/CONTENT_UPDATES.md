## 🔄 Circular Economy Emphasis

Updated content now clearly explains the circular economy model:

1. **Collection**: Waste2Pay RVM gathers plastic bottles
2. **Recognition**: Machine identifies and sorts waste
3. **Reward**: Users receive digital points
4. **Processing**: Partnership with Paste Lab for recycling
5. **Creation**: New products made from recycled plastic
6. **Redemption**: Users exchange points for products
7. **Reuse**: Products used repeatedly, reducing new plastic demand
